Get emergency help now

Trouble breathing or swallowing, swelling of the tongue or throat, fainting, or confusion can signal a severe reaction.

＋

Seek urgent medical advice

Skin that is hot, painful, swollen, rapidly spreading, blistering, or producing pus—especially with fever or feeling unwell—needs prompt assessment.

♡

Keep care gentle

Wash hands before touching the area. Keep it clean, avoid scratching or squeezing, and pause any new product that seems to trigger irritation.

Guidance: American Academy of Dermatology · NHS · rash warning signs · cellulitis · anaphylaxis. Keep this guide accessible offline.
